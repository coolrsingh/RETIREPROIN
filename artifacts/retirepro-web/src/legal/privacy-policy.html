4. Legal Basis — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

RetirePro processes your personal data under the following legal bases as defined in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), which governs the processing of digital personal data in India:

  • Consent (Section 6): You provide explicit, informed, unambiguous consent via the checkbox in our calculator form before submitting any personal data. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see Section 12).
  • Legitimate Uses (Section 7): We process certain data (e.g. anonymous analytics) for legitimate business purposes that do not override your fundamental rights.
  • Legal Obligation: We may process data where required by Indian law or a lawful order of a competent authority.

As a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, we are responsible for ensuring that your personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest: Database records are encrypted at rest via Supabase
  • Access controls: Row-level security ensures users can only access their own data
  • No payment data stored: Card details are handled exclusively by Razorpay and never touch our servers
  • Passwords: We use magic-link authentication — no passwords are stored

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required under the DPDP Act.

8. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal (i.e. the person whose data we process), you have the following rights under the DPDP Act:

  • Right to Access (Section 11): Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed.
  • Right to Correction (Section 12): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Section 12): Request deletion of your personal data. We will erase all your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6): Withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see Section 12 below).
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@retirepro.in or use the contact details in Section 12. We will respond within 30 days.
