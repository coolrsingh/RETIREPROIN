# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing landing page (`/` for unauthenticated users)
- Free planner and guest preview pages (`/free-plan`, `/plan/preview`)
- Blog index and blog article routes (`/blog`, `/blog/**`)
- FAQ page (`/faq`)
- Legal/compliance pages (`/privacy-policy`, `/disclaimer`, `/refund-policy`, `/terms-and-conditions`)
- Crawlability assets (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, favicon, Open Graph assets)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard and plan management routes (`/plan`, `/plan/:id` when saved, `/settings/crm`, `/leads`)
- Internal/admin CRM workflows
- Development-only artifacts and mockup sandbox
- Mobile app artifacts

## Target audience
- People in India planning retirement, including salaried professionals and higher-income households.

## Primary keywords
- retirement calculator India
- retirement planning India
- retirement corpus calculator India
- how much money to retire in India
- NPS vs PPF vs SIP retirement
